1. The Core Innovation: Fixed Minting with Dynamic Unallocated Lockup

To maintain 1:1 Physical Area Integrity (1 Acre = 40,468,564 $BMCM Tokens ALWAYS) while ensuring 100% fair monetary compensation for Landowner A (₹10 Lakh land) vs. Landowner B (₹25 Lakh land), BhoomiMesh implements the Unallocated Token Treasury & Land Appreciation Unlocking Engine.

  1. The smart contract ALWAYS mints 40,468,564 $BMCM tokens for 1 Acre.
  2. Tokens are allocated to the Landowner (70%) and Protocol Treasury (30%) strictly in proportion to the land's actual appraised market value.
  3. All remaining difference tokens are locked in the Protocol Unallocated Treasury Vault.
  4. As BhoomiMesh develops the raw land into commercial warehousing or solar farms (increasing land value from ₹10L to ₹25L to ₹40L), Unallocated Tokens are dynamically UNLOCKED on-chain and paid out to the Landowner (70%) and Protocol (30%) as a Development Dividend!
