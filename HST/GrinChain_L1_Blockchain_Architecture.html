1. Executive Summary: Why GrinRex Built GrinChain L1

Building a Sovereign Layer-1 RWA Blockchain (GrinChain) elevates GrinRex from a standard token on someone else's network to a national digital infrastructure protocol for real-world assets.

While public blockchains like Ethereum or Polygon suffer from gas fee spikes, network congestion, and lack of built-in regulatory compliance, GrinChain is purpose-built for Indian land registries, Sub-Registrar Offices (SRO), satellite LiDAR surveys, and cross-border RWA trading.
