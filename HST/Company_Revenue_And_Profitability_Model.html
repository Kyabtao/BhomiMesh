🎯 Executive Summary: How the Company Generates Millions in Profit

A critical business question is: "If 100% of net lease rent is distributed to stakers or used for buyback & burn, and the company leaves its 30% treasury tokens unstaked, how does the company/founders make money and corporate profit?"

The answer lies in the Multi-Layered Protocol Monetization Architecture. The company (BhoomiReserve SPV Pvt Ltd & GrinRex Technologies Ltd) does NOT rely on drawing tenant cash rent. Instead, it operates like a traditional Real Estate Asset Manager (REIT) and L1 Blockchain Infrastructure Operator, earning 5 high-margin corporate revenue streams:

🏢 1. Stream 1: Property Management & SPV Administration Fee (10% – 15%)

Before net lease rent is deposited into the Staking Dividend Vault, the company deducts a standard 10% to 15% Property Management & Administration Fee from gross rental collections for operating the physical assets, managing e-commerce tenants, handling Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) filings, and maintaining 24/7 satellite GPS fencing.

Gross Monthly Rent = ₹1.00 Crore / month (₹12.0 Crores / year)
Company Management Fee (12%) = ₹12.0 Lakhs / month (₹1.44 Crores / year DIRECT CORPORATE INCOME)
Net Rent to Staker Vault (88%) = ₹88.0 Lakhs / month (₹10.56 Crores / year)

🎯 Result: The company earns ₹1.44 Crores/year in steady corporate cash revenue at 120 Acres, scaling to ₹105+ Crores/year at 2,500 Acres!

⚡ 2. Streams 2 to 5: Blockchain, Royalties & Treasury Appreciation

Stream 2: GrinRex L1 Gas Fees

The company operates primary Genesis Validator Nodes on GrinRex L1. Earning 50% of network gas fees in $GR generates ₹3.50 Crores to ₹15.0 Crores / year in high-margin gas yield.

Stream 3: 30% Treasury OTC Sales

As $BMCM token price grows from ₹0.247 to ₹4.448, the company's 30% treasury grows to hundreds of crores in value. The company executes small Over-The-Counter (OTC) sales to institutional VC funds under 24-month lockups.

Stream 4: 1.5% Secondary Royalties

Every token trade on exchanges or deed redemption charges a 1.5% royalty. On ₹100 Crores annual trading volume, the company earns ₹1.0 Crore / year in pure protocol royalties.

Stream 5: Sub-Token Royalties

10% of ALL transaction fees from specialized sub-tokens ($GAME-REX, $SOCIAL-REX, $BIZ-REX) flow directly back to corporate profits!
