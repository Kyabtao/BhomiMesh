1. The Core Solution: Valuation-Weighted Token Minting

In physical real estate, 1 Acre of prime highway land (worth ₹25 Lakhs or ₹40 Lakhs) is fundamentally more valuable than 1 Acre of deep rural interior land (worth ₹10 Lakhs).

If a protocol mints the exact same fixed number of tokens for 1 Acre regardless of land value, prime landowners are unfairly under-compensated, while low-value landowners are over-compensated.

To solve this, BhoomiMesh implements the Valuation-Weighted Land Lease Tokenization Engine. Under this protocol:

  1. Total tokens minted for a land plot are calculated based on Actual Appraised Land Value (₹) relative to a Base Reference Benchmark Land Value (₹40 Lakhs / Acre).
  2. The 70% Landowner / 30% Protocol Treasury Split is applied to the Valuation-Weighted Token Pool, ensuring 100% mathematical fairness for every landowner across India!

2. The Valuation-Weighted Tokenization Formulas

Valuation Ratio (Rv) = Actual Appraised Land Value per Acre (₹) ÷ Base Benchmark Land Value (₹40 Lakhs)

Total Tokens Minted = Rv × (Acreage × 40,468,564 $BMCM)

Landowner Allocation (70%) = 0.70 × Total Tokens Minted

Protocol Treasury Allocation (30%) = 0.30 × Total Tokens Minted

🛠️ 4. Independent Valuation & Title Verification Workflow

  1. Step 1: IBBI Registered Valuer Appraisal: An independent Insolvency and Bankruptcy Board of India (IBBI) certified valuer conducts an on-site inspection and issues an official appraisal report.
  2. Step 2: Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) Circle Rate Benchmark: The valuation report is cross-checked against official state government Circle Rates / Ready Reckoner Rates.
  3. Step 3: On-Chain Valuation Oracle Publishing: The appraised land value (e.g. ₹25 Lakhs) is published on-chain via a Decentralized Valuation Oracle.
  4. Step 4: Smart Contract Auto-Minting: The LandownerLeasePortal.sol smart contract reads the verified valuation and automatically mints the exact valuation-weighted token pool (25,292,852 $BMCM for ₹25L land), splitting it 70% to the landowner's wallet and 30% to the treasury.
