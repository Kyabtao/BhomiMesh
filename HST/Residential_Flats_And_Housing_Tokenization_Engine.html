To solve this, BhoomiMesh introduces the HomeFlex Residential Housing Tokenization Engine ($SMART-HOMES).

  3. BhoomiMesh upgrades the flat into a Smart Managed Corporate Suite / Executive Short-Term Stay, boosting rental yields from 3% up to 12% – 15% APY!
